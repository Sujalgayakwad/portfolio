Beyond the CV: Who I Am & What Drives Me

A concise look into my cybersecurity interests, active technical exploration, and current research focus.

I am a security-focused technologist with a deep curiosity for understanding how protocols break and how defensive architectures withstand modern adversaries. Rather than viewing security as an afterthought, I approach engineering with a security-first, zero-trust mindset.

My core technical fascination lies in wireless radio frequency auditing, rogue access point heuristics, and password entropy thermodynamics. I enjoy dissecting 802.11 spectrum traffic, understanding the cryptographic nuances of WPA2 vs WPA3-SAE, and developing native operating system tools that turn passive network reconnaissance into actionable threat scoring.

What I am currently building & learning: Deepening my hands-on practice in memory forensics, threat hunting with Zeek & Suricata, and building automated security verification pipelines that combine LLM vector indexing with structured vulnerability feeds.
